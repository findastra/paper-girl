What color is this note?

Hearing colors is called chromesthesia, a kind of synesthesia where sounds bring up colors. This website collects the quiz results for everyone who takes the test.

Instructions

Play the piano note, then pick the color it feels like. Afterwards, compare your colors with other players and with published studies. A game is 12 notes, one for every piano key in an octave.

Editorial Note

There is no real right answer: people with chromesthesia rarely agree with each other, so the studies under "Compare and history" are things to compare with, not answer keys. One pattern most people share, with or without synesthesia, is that higher notes feel lighter (Ward, Huckstep and Tsakanikos, 2006). The crowd average uses each person's latest answer for each note.
